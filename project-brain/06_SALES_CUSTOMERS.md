# Sales & Customers

## Pricing (Phase 1 target)
- Pakistan: PKR 2,500–5,000/month

## Ideal customer profile
Small/solo digital agencies, web dev shops, software houses currently selling through WhatsApp + spreadsheets, with no CRM or priced out of HubSpot.
